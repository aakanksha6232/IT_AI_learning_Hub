# 🤖 AI Learning Roadmap — Role-Based Guide for Your Team

> **Team:** Business Analysts · Testers/QA · Developers · Leaders  
> **Baseline:** Casual ChatGPT/Copilot users  
> **Commitment:** 2–3 hrs/week  
> **Tech Stack:** .NET · Python · JS/TS · Java

---

## 📋 How to Use This Roadmap

| Step | Action |
|------|--------|
| **1** | **Everyone** starts with Phase 1 — All Roles (Weeks 1–4). This is shared AI literacy for the whole team. |
| **2** | From Week 5, navigate to your role track: BA · Tester · Developer · Leader. |
| **3** | Focus on 🔴 **MUST** items first. Complete all of them before touching 🟡 Good to Have. |
| **4** | Each Phase ends with a **Capstone ✅** exercise. Do not skip these — they solidify the learning. |
| **5** | Revisit this roadmap with your team monthly. Use it to track where everyone is. |
