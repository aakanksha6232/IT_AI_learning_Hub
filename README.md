# 🤖 AI Learning Hub

Welcome to the **IT - AI Learning Hub** – a centralized repository for our department to share knowledge, resources, and learning related to Artificial Intelligence.

---

## 🎯 Purpose
- Share AI learning resources (blogs, courses, videos)
- Encourage knowledge sharing across teams
- Track latest trends and innovations in AI
- Build a collaborative learning culture

---

## 📚 What You'll Find Here
- 📄 Blogs and articles
- 🎓 Courses (free & paid)
- 🛠 AI tools and platforms
- 📊 Datasets
- 🌍 Real-world use cases
- 📅 Weekly AI updates

---

## 👥 Who Can Contribute?
Everyone in the department is encouraged to contribute!

---

## 🚀 How to Contribute
1. Fork this repository
2. Add your resource in the appropriate folder
3. Follow the template
4. Submit a Pull Request

---

## ⭐ Contribution Guidelines
- Ensure links are valid
- Avoid duplicate entries
- Add short descriptions
- Categorize properly

---

Let's learn and grow together! 🚀
